Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Add items on the Menu page, open your Cart, choose collect or delivery and place the order. You pay when you collect or when it is delivered.

How much is delivery?

Delivery is R50, and free when your order is R500 or more. You can also collect in store for free.

How do promo codes work?

Copy a code from the Promotions page, paste it in the promo box in your Cart and press Apply. Each code has its own minimum spend.

How do I earn loyalty points?

You earn 1 point for every R10 you spend. Points are added once your order has been collected. Redeem them for vouchers on the Loyalty Rewards page.

Can I cancel or change an order?

Yes, as long as it has not been collected. Send a support ticket with your order number or call the shop.

A product is sold out. When will it be back?

Message us on WhatsApp or send a ticket and we will let you know when we restock.
